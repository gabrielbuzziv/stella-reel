# Edit rules

The decisions she makes sentence by sentence. Looks and numbers live in [FORMAT.md](FORMAT.md); this file says
what to do with each piece of the script. Work through the edited transcript one sentence at a time and record a
decision for every column of the plan ([PLAN.md](PLAN.md)).

## Shots

- **Cut at every sentence start.** Join two sentences in one shot only when the second is a 1-3 word tag of the
  first ("…e eu levei 2 minutos." stays with its claim). Cut mid-sentence only to hide a retake seam or to isolate
  the phrase that gets a punch-in.
- **Punch in (1.12-1.25x) on about one shot in four**, usually a single shot between base shots, on:
  - the punchline, the opinion, the second half of a contrast ("E eu prefiro o gringo.", "Strike.");
  - a voiced quote or another person's voice — quote on the punch-in, her answer back at base (the excuses in
    De_htrfxLP9 alternate this way);
  - insider or warning lines ("Presta atenção…", "Teste A/B");
  - the reveal of a proof she is pointing at (1.35x while the story screenshot is up);
  - the last CTA line (7 of 32 end on a punch-in);
  - comedy timing (De3vDCVObjG, a comment reply played for laughs, punches in on half its shots).
- **Stay at base** for explanations, list items being read, and emotional confessions (long holds on her face).
- Use the range across the video: base 1.0, regular punch 1.15-1.2, strong punch 1.25-1.35 for the biggest moments.

## What becomes text

Go down this list for each sentence; the first match decides.

1. **Hook** — hook serif, 1-3 lines with the claim (number on its own line or in BIG caps), then the add-on in
   handwriting, typed on its spoken words. Stays until the cut after the hook.
2. **A number or result** — headline with the figure as digits ("R$ 3.881 em 48h", "487 vendas", "12 minutos/dia"),
   plus the proof screenshot (Overlays). Money lines may get a 💵💵💵 row under them.
3. **An announced structure** — "4 passos", "3 filtros", "2 coisas que eu nunca faço"; then each item's label when
   spoken, numbered ("1.", keycaps 1️⃣ 2️⃣ 3️⃣). Short items she keeps referring back to (steps of a process, a ❌
   checklist) accumulate under the previous ones and stay until the topic ends (25-35 s is normal); long items that
   each get their own explanation replace one another, one per shot.
4. **A named concept** — 1-3 words as its own headline: "Arrogância", "Posição segura", "Justificativa",
   "Curadoria = Seguidor qualificado".
5. **A contrast** — "❌ X" then "✅ Y" lines, or "Estar certa ✅ / Dinheiro ❌".
6. **A voiced quote** — in curly quotes: bold italic for what others say ("“Me ensina a fazer o que você faz?”"),
   handwriting typed for her inner voice and for fill-in templates ("“Sou mãe de dois e trabalho na hora da soneca.”").
   Long quotes she reads (an offer, a message) get the giant quote marks around her face instead of text.
7. **Series tag** — series card: bold italic label "Episódio N" + typed subtitle with the series title.
8. **CTA** — the instruction with the keyword set apart: "Para saber mais, comenta “IMERSÃO”", "Pra receber é só me
   pedir", "Me segue pra acompanhar ✅", "Roteiro 👇".
9. **Anything else** — leave the screen clean.

Wording: her spoken words, compressed (drop articles and fillers), up to ~5 words per line, 1-3 lines; numbers in
digits; at most one CAPS word for emphasis ("QUANTOS te seguem", "Tenta ENSINAR"); "…" to carry one sentence across
two consecutive headlines ("Em 1 mês…" → "…e em 72 horas"); PT-BR spelling and accents exact.

**Clean screen** (no headline) for: scene-setting in a story, the reasoning between two key terms, the rebuttal in a
voiced dialogue, anaphora and litanies ("Todo mundo podia… menos eu"), emotional confessions, a sentence that repeats
what is already on screen, short transitions ("Só que tem uma coisa."). The running
caption word stays on throughout.

## Overlays

| What she says | What appears | Where |
|---|---|---|
| A number she achieved | the real screenshot of its source (Insights, sales dashboard, statement), cropped to the figure, red ellipse/arrow on the figure on the spoken number | top band when the headline is short, otherwise chest band |
| Followers / "esse perfil" | profile header crop, follower count circled | chest band (or top) |
| "Esse vídeo" / a reel | that reel as a playing vertical card | free side, next to the face |
| A person by name | cut-out photo, one per name as spoken (+ 💬 if they "said" something) | top band |
| "Me perguntaram / comentaram" | the real DM or comment screenshot; a cascade of many for volume | top band, or a cascade over the free half for 1-2 s |
| Steps inside an app | full-screen screen recording, 1-2 s per step, red arrow on each button | full frame |
| A message she dissects | crop of the line being discussed at the top, red underline/ellipse on the exact word as she names it; headline below it at forehead height | top band |
| A tool or product | app pill (icon + name) or product cover | top band or free side |
| An idiom, joke or drama beat | meme still or a 1-2 s clip that takes the words literally ("pulo do gato" → a cat jumping) | above the head |
| A list of concrete nouns | emoji row, one emoji per noun as spoken | top band |
| A choice / setting | mock toggle or checkbox list ("☐ Perfil atual / ☐ Perfil novo") | top band or chest band |

- One overlay group at a time: the next one replaces the previous (a screenshot plus its headline is one group).
- Every proof is the real artifact. Blur third parties' names and photos in DMs and comments (your call: hers are
  often left visible).
- The face stays visible; full-screen inserts last only as long as the steps they show.

## Emoji

- **Bullets** at line start: ❌ ✅ ⚠️ ‼️ ▪️ ☐, keycaps 1️⃣ 2️⃣ 3️⃣, ➡️ ⬆️ ⬇️.
- **Reaction** at line end, one per headline at most: 😬 🤯 😔 🫠 😱 🥹 🤫 😌 👇 ✅.
- **Emoji-only beat**: a short phrase of concrete nouns becomes a row of emoji with no words ("Amanhã com criança
  pequena é um caos" → 👶☀️🏠⚡; "curte, salva e vai embora" → ❤️📌👋), each popping on its word.
- **Equation**: 🙍‍♀️ + 📄 = 📒 · 📦 → ⏳.
- **Word swap** inside a text line: "o ❤️ da estratégia", "Um deles com uma 🐝".
- Abstract statements get no emoji.

## Sound

Use her exact sounds from [assets/sfx](assets/sfx); [FORMAT.md](FORMAT.md#sound) says what each one is and how the
levels are measured. Every file starts a few ms before its hit, so the element goes on the graphic's frame.

| Event | Sound | Where it goes | Level vs voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frame 0 | boom | at frame 0; its peak lands ~0.32 s later | +1.9 dB |
| New headline or overlay (default) | click | on the text-in frame | -12.4 dB |
| An image or screen entering | click (the boom for the biggest entrance) | on its first frame | -12.4 dB |
| Typewriter text | typing: a continuous keystroke burst, never a single key | from the first typed character to the last. Start the file from its beginning on every reveal and trim it where the reveal ends | -8.1 dB |
| Each emoji or list item of a build, each card of a cascade | tick | on each item's frame | -8.8 dB |
| Number, money, result | the click on its text, then the ding (or coins / cash register, once or twice per video) | ding ~0.35 s after the click | -11.7 dB |
| ❌ item, failure | thud | on the ❌ line's frame | -1.5 to -2 dB |
| The single strongest negative or ironic beat | vine boom | at most once | — |
| A word the screen spells out ("*cri cri cri*", "R$") | that literal sound | on the word | to taste, under the voice |

- **Typing lasts as long as the reveal**: characters ÷ the typewriter speed. Hers is ~25-30 chars/s; the Edits
  typewriter runs ~20 chars/s, so a 30-character line types for 1.5 s.
- No new graphic, no sound: plain cuts, punch-ins and clean stretches stay dry.
- When two graphics land within 0.25 s, one sound.
- SFX density is seasoning, not structure: audible SFX run 10-42 per minute across her edits, and one of her
  strongest videos (DepAMjBxWIT) has almost none. Keep the opening boom and the ❌ thuds; scale the rest.
